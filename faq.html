Questions about Partsong

Short answers to common questions. Something else? Use Report a problem in Partsong, or email bugs@partsong.app.

What is Partsong?

A free web app for choir singers. You photograph or open choral sheet music, Partsong reads it, and plays just your part (soprano, alto, tenor, bass or any other line) with the score following along. Watch the 1½-minute video to see it.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, adverts or payments. Partsong is free software (AGPL).

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser at partsong.app. On Android and computers you can add it to your home screen or desktop like an app, and it then works offline.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Scores shared from Partsong open and play there. Reading music from photos or PDFs is less reliable on iPhones and iPads, because Safari gives web pages less memory; if it doesn't work, read the music on a computer or Android phone and send yourself a link.

What music can it read?

Printed choral music: hymns, anthems, carols, part-songs, from photos or PDFs. Clean, straight pages read best. Handwritten music and guitar tablature aren't supported.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. The music is read on your device. Only if you share a score as a link is it uploaded, encrypted on your device first, so only people with the link can open it.

What are the red lines before it reads the music?

They mark where Partsong thinks each line of music (system) ends. If one is in the wrong place, tap between two staves to add or remove it, then tap Next.

My part moves between staves. What do I do?

Use Mark your part on the music and tap the staff you sing in each system. Where two voices share a staff, tap once for the upper voice and again for the lower.

Can I slow it down or repeat a passage?

Yes. Set the tempo, choose a range of bars and tick Loop. The Other parts slider sets how loud the other voices are.

Can I share a score with my choir?

Yes. Send a link to another singer gives them the score ready to practise; Send a recording of my part makes an audio file; Save as a PDF file saves the score.

Can I share music that's in copyright?

Only if you have permission. Sheet music is often in copyright: share scores that are out of copyright, or copies your choir is licensed to make for its singers. Partsong asks before a score is first shared, and when someone sends you one, add it only if you're allowed to use it. If you're unsure, ask your choir or the publisher. More in the terms.
