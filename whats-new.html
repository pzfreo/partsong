Partsong

What's new

The changes you'd notice, newest first. Versions in between had fixes behind the scenes. Your version is shown at the top of Partsong, next to "beta".

v364 26 November 2026

  • A photo of two pages side by side, such as an open book, or of a page set in two columns, is now read as two pages in the right order. Before, the two pages came out mixed into one muddled part.

v361 25 November 2026

  • Opened inside another app, such as the Google app, Instagram or Facebook, Partsong suggests opening it in Safari or Chrome instead, and shows how, with a Copy link button. Music reads faster there, and your scores stay in one place.

v357 25 November 2026

  • When a new version is ready, Partsong says which version it is and which you have, and What's new shows what's changed before you update. This page lists every version's changes.

v351 25 November 2026

  • Large photos open more reliably on iPhones and iPads that are short of memory.

v347 24 November 2026

  • Scanned PDFs now read properly. These are PDFs made from a scan or photocopy, like many older CPDL and IMSLP editions. Some used to come out as blank pages, and Partsong said it couldn't find any music.

v344 24 November 2026

  • Landscape pages printed in two columns no longer stop the reader.

v342 23 November 2026

  • Open CPDL's MusicXML files. Where a CPDL edition offers a MusicXML (.mxl) file, download that instead of the PDF and tap Open a MusicXML file. Partsong goes straight to practice, with every note exactly as the editor wrote it and no reading needed.
  • The red line follows the music smoothly, note by note, and no longer stops partway through a piece.

v334 23 November 2026

  • On iPhones, reading in low-memory mode is about three times faster.

v323 21 November 2026

  • On iPhones and iPads that are short of memory, reads that used to fail now finish, with Partsong using less memory.

v321 21 November 2026

  • When a read goes wrong, Partsong sends us a short record of what failed, so we can fix it. It never includes your music, its name or your photos; the privacy page has the details.

v306 15 November 2026

  • Send a recording of my part now records the whole piece, start to finish, whichever bars you've set Play to.

v305 14 November 2026

  • A new video shows how to choose your part in harder scores (Help).

v298 14 November 2026

  • Report a problem can now send the score or pages with your email, so we can see exactly what went wrong.

v291 13 November 2026

  • The getting-started video plays right on the start screen.

v288 12 November 2026

  • If your phone closes Partsong while it's reading, it carries on where it left off, using less memory.

v266 11 November 2026

  • Before you share a score, Partsong asks once whether you're allowed to share that music. Shared links last a month.
