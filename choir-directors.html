Partsong for choir directors

Most rehearsal time goes on notes that singers could have learnt at home, if they had a way to hear their part. Partsong gives every singer that, from the music you already hand out.

Open Partsong

How your singers use it

They open partsong.app on their phone or computer, photograph the music or open your PDF, and choose their part. Partsong plays just that line, with the score following along, at whatever speed they need. There's nothing to buy, no account to make, and nothing to install (on Android it can be added to the home screen like an app).

Read it once, share it with everyone

You, or one confident singer, can read a piece into Partsong and check it, then use Send a link to another singer. Everyone who opens the link gets the score ready to practise, without reading it themselves. Shared scores are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded, and only people with the link can open them.

A marked-up copy for each part

In Your scores, the copy button next to a score makes a copy with the same music, marks and settings, ready to rename. Make one each for Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass, mark that part on the music and set its playback, and each version stays separate. Then send each section the link to its own.

Recordings for those who just want to listen

Send a recording of my part makes an audio file of one part, which you can send to a section that would rather just press play.

Good to know

  • Partsong reads printed choral music: hymns, anthems, carols, part-songs. Clean, straight pages read best; handwritten music isn't supported.
  • It checks the lines of music (systems) with you before reading, and singers can mark their part on the music where the layout changes from page to page.
  • Reading the music works best on Android phones and computers. On iPhones and iPads reading is less reliable, but shared scores open and play there.
  • Only share music you're allowed to copy: public-domain editions, or music your choir has the right to reproduce.
