Partsong

Rehearsal tracks for any piece

Rehearsal tracks (sometimes called part-predominant or learning tracks) play the whole piece with your part brought forward. They're the quickest way to learn notes, but they only exist for popular pieces, and buying them for a whole season's music adds up. Partsong makes one for whatever your choir is singing, from the sheet music itself.

Open Partsong

Your part, as loud as you need it

Choose your part and the Other parts slider sets how loud everyone else is: down to nothing to learn your notes, up to full to practise holding your line against the harmony.

At your speed, a few bars at a time

Slow the tempo right down (from 30 beats a minute) while you learn a tricky passage, then bring it back up. Set From bar and to and tick Loop to go over the same few bars until they stick. The score follows along as it plays, so you see where you are.

A sound that suits you

Play your part on piano, organ or a sung "voice" sound. Tenors reading a treble clef can have their part played an octave lower, as it's sung.

Take the track with you

Send a recording of my part makes an audio file of your part with the settings you chose, to play in the car, on a walk, or anywhere without the app.

What you need

The sheet music: a photo of each page, or the PDF. Partsong reads printed choral music on your device, and nothing is uploaded. It's free, with no account.
