Practise CPDL scores with Partsong

CPDL, the Choral Public Domain Library, has free editions of tens of thousands of choral works, from Tallis to hymn tunes. Partsong turns any of those PDFs into a practice track for your part.

Open Partsong

From CPDL to your part in three steps

  1. Download the PDF of the edition your choir uses from its CPDL page.
  2. Open it in Partsong (Open PDF), check the lines of music it found, and tap Next to read it.
  3. Choose your part and press play.

Try one now

Partsong's sample score is Tallis's If ye love me, from CPDL: tap Try a sample score on Partsong's home screen to hear how it works before you download anything.

Which editions read best

CPDL editions typeset with notation software (most of them) read well. Scans of old printed editions work if the pages are clean and straight; handwritten editions don't. Where the voices change staff from page to page, mark your part on the music.

Share it with your choir

CPDL editions are free to copy, so once one singer has read a piece, Send a link to another singer gives everyone the score ready to practise.
